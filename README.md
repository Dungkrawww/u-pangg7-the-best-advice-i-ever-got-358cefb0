Entry for the make.fun competition "The Best Advice I Ever Got" ($2 in USDC).

The entry sentence (124 characters):

> My grandmother said, "Eat before you are hungry, sleep before you are tired, and never borrow trouble you have not met yet."

Why it lands: a real voice recalling a real moment — three small, specific habits that add up to a whole philosophy of not suffering twice. Plain, earned, and true beyond the moment it came from.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
